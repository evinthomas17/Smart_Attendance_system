For your Smart Attendance System, these 8 Django apps can be organized like this:

#	Django App	Main Function	Simple Explanation
1	accounts	Authentication & User Access	Handles login, logout, JWT, passwords, roles and permissions. It does not register students/faculty.
2	adminpanel	Admin Operations	Provides the Admin Dashboard. Admin can register/manage students and faculty, manage system data, and view reports.
3	students	Student Management	Stores and manages student profiles and student-specific information.
4	faculty	Faculty Management	Stores and manages faculty profiles and faculty-specific information.
5	academics	Academic Management	Handles departments, courses, subjects, semesters, classes and timetables.
6	classrooms	Classroom Management	Manages classrooms/labs, their details, capacity, location and classroom assignments.
7	devices	ESP32/BLE Management	Manages ESP32 devices, BLE identifiers, classroom-device mapping and device status.
8	attendance	Attendance Management	Handles attendance sessions, verification, attendance records, status and reports.
1. accounts — Authentication

Purpose: Who can access the system?

Handles:

Login
Logout
JWT authentication
Access/refresh tokens
Password change/reset
User roles
Permissions

Roles:

ADMIN
FACULTY
STUDENT

Important: Student and faculty registration is not handled here.

2. adminpanel — Administration

Purpose: What can the administrator do?

The Admin Dashboard is handled here.

Admin can:

Register students
Register faculty
Manage students
Manage faculty
Manage departments/subjects
Manage classrooms
Manage ESP32 devices
View attendance reports
View dashboard statistics

Think of it as the control center of the system.

3. students — Student Management

Purpose: Everything specifically related to students.

Stores information such as:

Student ID
Name
Email
Register Number
Department
Course
Semester
Phone
Profile information

The adminpanel can create/manage students, but the actual Student model and student-related data belong here.

4. faculty — Faculty Management

Purpose: Everything specifically related to faculty.

Stores:

Faculty ID
Name
Email
Department
Designation
Phone
Profile information

Again, adminpanel manages faculty, while the actual Faculty model/data belongs here.

5. academics — Academic Management

Purpose: Defines the academic structure of the college.

For example:

Department
   ↓
Course
   ↓
Semester
   ↓
Subject
   ↓
Timetable

It can manage:

Departments
Courses
Semesters
Subjects
Academic years
Timetables
Class/subject assignments
6. classrooms — Classroom Management

Purpose: Manages physical classrooms and labs.

For example:

Classroom
├── Room number
├── Building
├── Floor
├── Capacity
├── Latitude
├── Longitude
└── Assigned ESP32 device

This app is particularly important for your GPS verification and classroom-based attendance.

7. devices — ESP32/BLE Management

Purpose: Manages the hardware used for classroom verification.

Handles:

ESP32 registration
ESP32 unique ID
BLE information
Device status
Classroom ↔ ESP32 mapping
Expected device for attendance sessions

Example:

Classroom A101
      ↓
ESP32-001
      ↓
BLE verification
8. attendance — Attendance Management

Purpose: The core attendance processing system.

Handles:

Creating attendance sessions
Starting/ending sessions
Student attendance
GPS verification
BLE/ESP32 verification
PIN verification
QR verification
Face verification, if enabled
Time validation
Present/Late/Absent status
Attendance history
Attendance reports

A typical flow is:

Faculty
   ↓
Create Attendance Session
   ↓
Student attempts attendance
   ↓
Verify:
   ├── Login
   ├── Time
   ├── GPS
   ├── ESP32/BLE
   ├── PIN/QR
   └── Face (optional)
   ↓
Attendance Validation
   ↓
Present / Late / Rejected
   ↓
Attendance Record
Overall system
                    SMART ATTENDANCE SYSTEM
                              │
        ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
        │                     │                     │
    accounts              adminpanel            attendance
 Authentication          Admin Control         Attendance
        │                     │                     │
        │          ┌──────────┼──────────┐          │
        │          │          │          │          │
        ▼          ▼          ▼          ▼          ▼
     students   faculty   academics  classrooms   devices
Easy way to remember

accounts → Who can log in?
adminpanel → What can the admin manage?
students → Who are the students?
faculty → Who are the faculty?
academics → What is being taught?
classrooms → Where is it taught?
devices → Which ESP32 verifies the classroom?
attendance → Who attended and how was it verified?
